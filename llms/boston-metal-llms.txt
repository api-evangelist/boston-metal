# Boston Metal

> Boston Metal is an industrial metals and materials company founded in 2013 and headquartered in Woburn, Massachusetts, with a wholly owned subsidiary and commercial plant in Brazil. It commercializes Molten Oxide Electrolysis (MOE), a one-step electrochemical process that uses clean electricity instead of fossil fuels to reduce iron ore into liquid metal — producing steel with no direct CO2 emissions — and to recover high-value critical metals such as niobium and tantalum from low-grade ore and mining waste.
